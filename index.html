About Me

I am a final year PhD student at the London School of Geometry and Number Theory (LSGNT) and Imperial College London, under the supervision of George Boxer and Fred Diamond.

I am interested in mod p coherent cohomology of Shimura varieties, p-adic and mod p modular forms, and Galois representations.

Research
